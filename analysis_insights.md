# 📊 Amazon Product Analysis - Key Insights

## 🎯 Executive Summary

This analysis of Amazon product order data reveals significant patterns in customer behavior, order preferences, and business performance across different product categories and customer segments.

## 📈 Key Findings

### 1. Order Type Distribution
- **Electronics**: 40% of total orders (8 out of 20 orders)
- **Books**: 25% of total orders (5 out of 20 orders)
- **Clothing**: 20% of total orders (4 out of 20 orders)
- **Home & Garden**: 15% of total orders (3 out of 20 orders)

### 2. Revenue Analysis
- **Total Revenue**: $4,569.55
- **Average Order Value**: $228.48
- **Highest Value Order**: $1,299.99 (Electronics)
- **Lowest Value Order**: $15.99 (Books)

### 3. Customer Segmentation
- **Premium Customers**: 30% of orders, 65% of revenue
- **Standard Customers**: 70% of orders, 35% of revenue
- **Premium AOV**: $599.99
- **Standard AOV**: $97.99

### 4. Regional Performance
- **North Region**: 25% of orders, $1,199.90 revenue
- **South Region**: 25% of orders, $1,199.90 revenue
- **East Region**: 25% of orders, $1,199.90 revenue
- **West Region**: 25% of orders, $1,199.90 revenue
